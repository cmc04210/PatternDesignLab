How to play

  1. A customer arrives with an order: a season, a budget, and sometimes a special request.
  2. Tap one part from each category – Tires, Exhaust, and Engine – to build the car.
  3. Watch the running total cost. Stay under budget!
  4. Hit Deliver before the timer runs out.
  5. Wrong build or too slow? You lose a wrench. Lose all three and the shift is over.

Faster deliveries and money saved under budget earn bonus points.
